1. The app

Zikirmatik is a dhikr counter and Quran reading app, offered free of charge. Your dhikrs, statistics and personal khatms are stored on your device; see the Privacy Policy for details.

2. Shared khatm groups

Shared khatm groups let you divide the 30 juz among people you know. Groups are not public: only someone who knows the 6-character join code can join. Groups are never listed and cannot be searched for.

When you join a group, two pieces of information are visible to other members:

  • The group name, written by the person who created the group
  • The name you use in the group — a nickname is fine, your real name is not required

These two fields are user-generated content seen by other people, and the rules below apply to them.

3. Content rules

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and for abusive users. No text you enter — a group name, a nickname, or a report description — may contain:

  • Insults, profanity, threats, harassment or bullying
  • Hate speech, or demeaning others by religion, sect, race, gender, origin or disability
  • Obscene, sexual or violent expressions
  • Encouragement of illegal activity
  • Advertising, spam, scams or link spreading
  • Impersonation of another person
  • Personal details such as phone numbers or addresses, whether yours or someone else's
  • Copyrighted material belonging to others

In short: this app is used for worship. Remember that the name you type is seen by everyone in the group, including members you have never met.

4. Reporting and enforcement

Every group screen has a “Report group” link at the bottom. If you see an inappropriate group name or nickname, report it there and add a short description if you wish. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

Following a review we may:

  • Remove the content or delete the group entirely
  • Block the offending user's access to the shared khatm feature

In addition, the person who created a group can remove any member from the member list with “Remove”. A removed person cannot return unless they are given the code again, so if a member is disruptive, deleting the group is the surest remedy.

For urgent matters, or anything you cannot report from inside the app, write to zikirmatik.help@gmail.com.

5. Identity

Shared khatm requires no account and no sign-in. The app creates an anonymous identity that belongs to your device only and is not tied to your name; your actions in a group are matched to it. If you delete the app or change devices, that identity is lost: you can no longer reach your groups or manage a group you created.
