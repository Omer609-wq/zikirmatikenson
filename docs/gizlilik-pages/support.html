Frequently asked questions

How do I join a hatim group?

Tap the Community card on the home screen, open the Together tab, choose Join With Code and enter the 6-character code you were sent.

How do I create a hatim group and invite friends?

Go to Community → Together → Create Hatim Group. Tap the join code on the group screen to open the invite message and share it via WhatsApp, Telegram or Messages.

The daily reminder doesn’t arrive

  • Check that Settings → Daily reminder is on and the reminder time is right.
  • Make sure notifications are allowed. On iPhone: Settings → the app → Notifications. On Android: App info → Notifications.
  • If notifications arrive late on Android, allow Alarms & reminders for the app and remove battery-saving restrictions.

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

Your dhikr, counters, statistics and settings are stored only on your device; no account is needed. Deleting the app also deletes these records. On iPhone, if iCloud Backup is on, the data may be included in your device backup. Only shared hatim groups are stored on a server; see the Privacy Policy for details.
